USDC invoices · Solana devnet · on-chain escrow

Invoices that prove they were paid.

Paytruth moves USDC from one wallet to another through escrow. When a customer pays, the Solana program records it — so you never have to take anyone's word for it.

How it works

  1. 01

    Create an invoice

    Enter an amount and a short description. Paytruth opens an escrow account for it on Solana.

  2. 02

    Your customer pays into escrow

    They pay USDC straight into escrow. No account, no sign-up, no sharing card details.

  3. 03

    Anyone can verify on-chain

    A public page shows paid, claimed and expired straight from the program. Not from our server.
