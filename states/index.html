Invoice states

The invoice is open. Your customer can pay the amount due into escrow.

Expiring

The expiry time has passed and no one paid. The invoice can be marked expired.

Paid

The customer paid the full amount into escrow. Claim it to move the USDC to your wallet.

Refund due

The customer paid more than the amount due. The extra is held until it is refunded.

Refunded

The customer was paid back the extra amount. The amount due is ready to claim.

The amount due has been claimed. Once the vault is empty the invoice can be closed.
